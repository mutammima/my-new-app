DuoNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

DuoNotes is a private shared-notes app for two people. This policy describes what the app stores, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What DuoNotes stores

  • Your email address — used to sign you in and to let your partner link to you.
  • A display name — whatever you type. It appears on shared notes and in greetings.
  • Your notes — titles, body text, drawings, and any photos you add. Photos are embedded directly inside the note rather than stored separately.
  • Who you are linked to — the account you have connected as your partner.
  • Timestamps — when each note was created and last changed.

What DuoNotes does not collect

There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking of any kind in this app. It does not collect your location, your contacts, or an advertising identifier. No data is sold, shared, or handed to anyone for marketing.

Who can see your notes

  • You.
  • Your linked partner — but only for notes you explicitly choose to share. Notes you have not shared are visible to you alone. This is enforced by database access rules on the server, not only by the app.
  • Whoever operates this app's database. DuoNotes is run privately by one person on their own Supabase project. That operator has technical access to the database, as the operator of any self-hosted service does.

Encryption — plainly

Traffic between your phone and the server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Note contents are not yet encrypted at rest. They are stored as ordinary text in the database.

The note lock is a gate, not encryption. A PIN- or Face ID-locked note is hidden behind that check on your device — it is not scrambled. Anyone with database access could read it.

End-to-end encryption of note bodies is planned but not implemented. This policy will be updated when that changes, and not before.

What stays on your phone

  • A copy of your notes, so the app works without a signal.
  • Your app-lock PIN, stored as a salted hash in the iOS keychain. The PIN itself is never stored and never leaves your device.
  • Small preferences: pinned notes, folders, theme.

Deleting everything

Open Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, your profile, and every note you own from the server, and wipes the app's data from your phone. It cannot be undone. If you were linked to a partner, they keep their own notes and are simply unlinked.

Children

DuoNotes is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.
